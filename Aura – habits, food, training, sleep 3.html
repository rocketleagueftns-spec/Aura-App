Backup your data

Aura only lives in this browser. If you clear your browser data, switch phones, or use a different browser, you lose everything unless you save a backup first.

To back up: tap "Create backup code", then tap "Copy" and paste it somewhere safe, like a notes app.

To restore: paste a backup code you saved earlier, then tap Restore. This replaces everything currently in the app with what's in the code.
